## 📸 How to Use (4 Steps)

1. **Click +** button (bottom left)
2. **Select image** of skin
3. **Wait** 2-3 seconds
4. **View results** with recommendations

---

## ⚠️ Key Limitations

- 85%+ accuracy (not 100%)
- NOT a medical diagnosis
- Needs clear, focused images
- Similar conditions may confuse
- Use with dermatologist consultation

---

## 💡 Best Photography Tips

✅ Natural lighting  
✅ Clear, close-up photos  
✅ Good focus  
✅ Multiple angles  
✅ JPG/PNG format  
✅ See dermatologist to confirm
