📸 How to Analyze Your Skin

Step 1: Locate the + Button Look at the bottom left of the chat. Click the + button to open image upload.
Step 2: Select an Image Choose a clear photo of the skin area you want to analyze. Make sure lighting is good.
Step 3: Wait for Analysis The image will be uploaded and analyzed by our AI model (takes 2-3 seconds).
Step 4: View Results You'll see the detected condition, confidence score, and recommendations.

⚠️ Chatbot Limitations

🤖 AI Assistant Role: This chatbot uses general responses for most questions. For detailed skincare advice, ask about specific skin conditions or upload an image.
📷 Image Analysis Only: The AI works best with uploaded images. Try to provide clear, well-lit photos for accurate results.
💬 Limited Conversation: Chat responses are pre-defined. Ask about skin analysis or products for better results.

🔍 Model Limitations & Accuracy

Accuracy Range: The model achieves 85%+ accuracy on trained conditions. However, accuracy may vary based on image quality.
Not a Replacement: AI predictions are NOT a medical diagnosis. Always consult a dermatologist for professional evaluation.
Image Quality Matters: Poor lighting, angles, or resolution can affect accuracy. Clear, close-up photos work best.
Similar Conditions: Some conditions like acne and eczema can be confused if they're borderline. System flags uncertain predictions.
Single Photo: Diagnosis based on one image only. Multiple photos from different angles improve accuracy.
Unknown Conditions: The model may struggle with rare conditions or conditions it wasn't trained on.

✨ Tips for Best Results

  • Use natural lighting (avoid shadows)
  • Take clear, close-up photos of the affected area
  • Ensure the image is in focus
  • Provide multiple angles if possible
  • Use JPG or PNG format images
  • Consult a dermatologist for final diagnosis
